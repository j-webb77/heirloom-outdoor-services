About Heirloom

Rooted in Craftsmanship, Not Shortcuts

We're a landscaping company based in The Woodlands, Texas, built around three things we do exceptionally well: irrigation, hardscapes, and grass.

Every property in Montgomery County sits on different soil, different drainage, and different sun exposure. We walk the lot before we quote it, so the system we design actually matches the ground it's going into.

From the first trench to the final paver, the same crew stays on your job. That's how a landscape becomes an heirloom.
