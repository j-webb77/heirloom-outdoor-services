About Heirloom Outdoor Services

A locally owned landscaping company in The Woodlands, Texas, built on the belief that good outdoor work should outlast the people who installed it.

Our Story

Built Around the Ground We Work On

Heirloom Outdoor Services started with a simple frustration: too many landscaping crews in Montgomery County were installing systems designed for somebody else's soil, somebody else's sun, somebody else's water.

We built this company around the opposite approach. Before we quote a job, we walk the property. We look at the grade, the shade pattern, the drainage, and how the homeowner actually uses the yard. Then we design for that specific lot — not a template.

Our three core specialties are irrigation installation and sprinkler repair, hardscapes and pavers, and grass installation. We also handle landscape beds and drainage, because the work is only as good as the foundation underneath it.

The same crew that starts your job finishes your job. That's how a landscape becomes an heirloom.

How We Got Here

From One Truck to a Full Outdoor Crew

Heirloom has grown the slow way — by doing right by one property, then the next.

  1. 01

    The First Sprinkler Repair

    Started as a one-man operation repairing irrigation systems around Conroe — back when the phone number was the business card.

  2. 02

    Hardscapes Added

    Homeowners kept asking who could build the patios and walkways to match the irrigation work. So we learned it properly — base prep, compaction, and all.

  3. 03

    Full Outdoor Services

    Sod, drainage, and landscape beds rounded out the offering. One crew could now take a yard from bare dirt to finished — without handing you off to three different contractors.

  4. 04

    Serving All of Montgomery County

    Today we work throughout The Woodlands, Conroe, Magnolia, Spring, Willis, and Montgomery — still locally owned, still the same crew showing up on your property.
