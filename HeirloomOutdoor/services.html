Landscaping Services

Irrigation, hardscapes, sod, drainage, and landscape design — quoted on-site, in writing, before any work begins.

All services

Irrigation Installation

Zone-by-zone sprinkler design that matches your lawn's actual water needs. Proper head spacing, matched precipitation rates, and a controller you can actually understand.

  • Full system design & trenching
  • Drip zones for beds & trees
  • Smart controller setup

Sprinkler Repair

Broken heads, stuck valves, leaks at the backflow, or a controller that runs when it shouldn't — we diagnose fast and fix the cause.

  • Valve & solenoid replacement
  • Head & nozzle repair
  • Leak detection & pressure tuning

Hardscapes & Pavers

Patios, walkways, retaining walls, and fire features built on a properly compacted base — because in Texas clay, the base is the job.

  • Paver patios & walkways
  • Retaining & seat walls
  • Fire pits & outdoor living

Grass & Sod Installation

The right turf for your light and soil — St. Augustine, Bermuda, or Zoysia — graded, rolled, and watered in so it knits together fast.

  • Grading & soil prep
  • Sod supply & install
  • Establishment watering plan

Landscape Beds & Design

Bed reshaping, edging, native-friendly plantings, and fresh mulch that frames the architecture of your home instead of hiding it.

  • Bed shaping & edging
  • Shrub & ornamental install
  • Mulch & soil amendment

Drainage Solutions

Standing water after a storm is a grading problem. We correct the slope and move the water away from your foundation for good.

  • French drains & surface drains
  • Grading & swale correction
  • Downspout extensions
